## Why this Code exists

GT Overlander is more than an app. It is a community of overlanders, motorhomers, 4×4 travelers, vans and road adventurers who decided to travel together.

When a community grows, clear rules become part of caring for one another. This Code exists to make explicit what we expect from each other — and what we will not tolerate from anyone, under any circumstances.

This document is complementary to the Terms of Use. The Terms serve to legally protect the Platform and the Users. This Code is more about culture: the kind of people we want to be inside GT.

Summed up in one sentence: respect, honesty and contribution. Let's detail what that means in practice.

## 1. Principles of the GT Community

### Respect

Treat everyone the way you would like to be treated. The road connects very different people — backgrounds, ages, genders, religions, life choices. That diversity is the richness of the overlander community. Respectful behavior is the floor, not the ceiling.

### Honesty

The foundation of GT depends on the truth. When you validate a waypoint, describe a route or rate a Help Overlander, other people will make real decisions based on what you published. Honesty here is not just ethics — it is collective safety.

### Contribution

You use points registered by others, follow routes planned by others, are rescued by others in difficult moments. Whenever you can, contribute back — validate a point, describe a road you know, offer help when you are available. Community is a two-way street.

### Open curiosity

Every overlander has their own way of traveling — some like extreme off-road, others prefer an RV on paved roads, others travel in a converted van. There is no single "right way". Respect other people's choices and learn from the differences.

### Safety first

In any interaction, on any route, at any meetup, your safety and the safety of others come before anything else. If something does not feel right, back off. If you see someone at risk, help if it is safe to do so, or call in someone who can.

## 2. What we expect from you

  - Be respectful and courteous in all interactions, even in disagreements.

  - Share truthful information (waypoints, validations, routes, ratings).

  - Acknowledge when a piece of information is not certain (use "I don't know" if you have not verified it).

  - Give credit to those who deserve it (copied routes, shared photos).

  - Help when you can, without necessarily expecting anything in return.

  - Rate fairly — neither inflating ratings nor retaliating with unfair negative ratings.

  - Report content or behavior that violates these rules.

  - Respect consent — do not photograph, do not film, do not publish people without authorization.

  - Treat the road and the environment with care — leave no trace, do not litter, respect protected areas.

  - Take local rules seriously (camping, national parks, indigenous communities, private properties).

## 3. What we do not tolerate

### 3.1. Abusive behavior

  - Hate speech in any form — racism, xenophobia, homophobia, transphobia, sexism, ableism, religious intolerance.

  - Bullying, harassment, persecution (stalking) — of other Users or of GT moderators.

  - Threats, intimidation, blackmail, inducement to harmful behavior.

  - Non-consensual sharing of intimate images (revenge porn).

  - Sexually explicit content, especially involving minors.

### 3.2. Fraud and manipulation

  - Registration of fictitious waypoints to deceive the community.

  - False validations to earn XP or to harm competitors.

  - Ratings that are arranged, bought or driven by bad faith.

  - Creation of multiple accounts (sock puppets) to inflate reputation or to circumvent a ban.

  - Artificial engagement in ranking, achievements or highlights.

  - False listings on the Shopping or on Desapega (non-existent product, misleading description, bait pricing).

  - Attempts to defraud payments or to charge unauthorized amounts.

### 3.3. Illegal activities

  - Promotion, sale or advertising of weapons, drugs, wild animals, prohibited content.

  - Solicitation or offer of illegal services via Help Overlander or the Shopping.

  - Use of the Platform as a means of committing crimes against the person, against property or against the public administration.

  - Sharing of content that violates third parties' copyright or image rights.

### 3.4. Spam and noise

  - Repeated posting of identical content or of mass advertising.

  - Use of Help Overlander as a personal or business marketing channel.

  - Creation of waypoints with no relevance to the overlander universe.

  - Mass connections with no real purpose (mass following/unfollowing).

### 3.5. Breach of privacy

  - Doxxing — public exposure of personal information (phone number, address, ID document) of another User.

  - Sharing or sale of data extracted from the Platform.

  - Attempts to identify anonymous Users through social engineering.

## 4. How to report

If you witness or suffer behavior that violates this Code, report it. The report is confidential and does not require direct confrontation.

  - For specific content (photo, listing, comment, profile): use the "Report" button available on the item itself inside the Platform.

  - For sensitive cases (harassment, threat, serious fraud, criminal conduct): write directly to legal@gtoverlander.com.br, describing what happened in as much detail as possible.

  - For personal data protection matters (leak, misuse): contact dpo@gtoverlander.com.br.

Every report will be analyzed by the moderation team within a reasonable period, taking the severity into account. Urgent reports (involving risk to people) have priority.

Do not report in bad faith. False reports made with the purpose of harming third parties are also violations of this Code and may result in a sanction against the person who reported.

## 5. What happens in case of a violation

GT adopts a gradual and proportional policy, depending on the severity and the history:

  - Warning — for minor infractions or a first occurrence. Direct notification to the User, indicating the rule violated and providing guidance.

  - Content removal — when the content violates the rules. The User is informed of the removal and of the reason.

  - Temporary suspension — for repeated infractions or those of medium severity. The Account is blocked for a defined period.

  - Loss of status — loss of XP, achievements, badges, provider status in Help Overlander, position in rankings, editorial or Business highlights.

  - Permanent ban — for serious infractions, systematic abusive conduct, criminal behavior or multiple repeat infractions.

  - Referral to authorities — when the conduct constitutes a crime, GT may call in the Police, the Ministério Público (Brazil's Public Prosecutor's Office) or other competent authorities, providing data in accordance with the law.

Moderation decisions are made by GT's responsible team, based on these rules and on contextual analysis.

## 6. Right of appeal

If you believe that a sanction was unfair, you have the right to present a rebuttal (contraditório, the right to be heard and to contest the decision). To do so:

  - Send your statement to legal@gtoverlander.com.br within 30 (thirty) days counted from the notification.

  - Include your identification, a description of the case, the reasons why you understand that the decision should be reviewed, and any evidence that supports your position.

  - GT will analyze the appeal within a reasonable period (up to 30 business days) and will respond with a reasoned decision.

  - If the appeal is upheld, the sanction will be reversed and, when applicable, the lost elements (XP, achievements, content) will be restored.

The appeal is a good-faith channel. It does not serve to reopen decisions that have already been appealed, except in the case of a relevant new fact.

## 7. How this Code evolves

This document is a living one. As the GT community grows, new situations arise and rules need refinement.

GT commits to:

  - Review this Code periodically, in light of incidents that have occurred and patterns observed.

  - Communicate relevant changes through the official channels with reasonable advance notice.

  - Keep the version history available for consultation.

  - Consider community feedback in the evolution of the rules (suggestions can be sent to suporte@gtoverlander.com.br).

## 8. Final word

The road teaches us many things. One of the main ones is that we go farther together.

This Code is not a list of prohibitions — it is the design of a place where you can trust people, count on the community, find someone willing to help when a tire blows out in the middle of nowhere.

Welcome. And safe travels.
