By registering and using the Platform, you declare that you have read, understood and accepted this Policy. If you have any questions, our Data Processing Officer (DPO) is available by e-mail at dpo@gtoverlander.com.br.

## 2. Personal Data We Collect

We collect the following personal data, grouped by purpose:

### 2.1. Registration Data

  - Full name

  - E-mail

  - Password (stored in encrypted form — GT never accesses the password in plain text)

  - Phone number (optional)

  - Profile photo (optional)

  - Basic location (city, state — optional)

  - For a Business Account: corporate name or full name, CNPJ or CPF (the Brazilian taxpayer registration numbers for companies and for individuals, respectively), business address, verification contact

### 2.2. Platform Usage Data

  - Itineraries created, saved and carried out

  - Waypoints visited, validated, created or rated

  - Photos uploaded to the Platform

  - Listings published on the Shopping, including on Desapega

  - Ratings given and received

  - Requests and assistance carried out via Help Overlander

  - Connections with other Users (followers, following)

  - Achievements, XP, levels and rankings in GT Explorer

### 2.3. Geolocation Data

  - Approximate location by IP (accessed when you open the Platform)

  - Precise location by GPS (collected only with your express permission, in specific features such as Navigation Mode and Help Overlander)

  - History of visited points (linked to validations and records that you make)

### 2.4. Technical Data

  - IP address

  - Type and version of the browser, operating system and device

  - Unique device identifiers (when applicable)

  - Access logs (date, time, page accessed)

  - Application usage and performance data (error telemetry, performance metrics)

### 2.5. Payment Data

  - Type of Plan subscribed to and subscription history (start, renewals and cancellation)

  - Payment status (without storage by GT of card numbers — for subscriptions, payment is made in the app store; for Business Account services, in the contracted payment gateway, as per Section 4.1)

  - CPF or CNPJ for the issuance of the Nota Fiscal (NFS-e, the Brazilian electronic service invoice), for services contracted directly with GT

### 2.6. Communication Data

  - Messages exchanged with our support

  - Initial message sent to the provider in Help Overlander (free text, to remain recorded for audit purposes)

### 2.7. Blog Comment Data

  - To comment on a blog article, you must sign in with your Google Account. We use this login only to identify who commented and to reduce spam and automated comments, collecting the name, e-mail and public profile photo of your Google Account.

  - The name and the profile photo are publicly visible alongside the comment, after approval. The e-mail is never displayed publicly, and is used only for internal moderation.

  - We do not use this login for any other purpose (we do not create an account on the Platform, we do not send marketing e-mail based on it, we do not link it to other data that you already have with GT).

  - Every comment goes through moderation (automatic and manual) before becoming visible to other readers.

## 4. Data Sharing

GT shares personal data with third parties only when strictly necessary to operate the Platform, to comply with legal obligations or to respond to substantiated requests. These third parties are, as the case may be, classified as Processors (they act on our behalf) or Independent Controllers.

### 4.1. Processors and Service Providers

  - Microsoft Azure — cloud hosting provider (servers, database, storage). Datacenters in Brazil and, for specific services, in the United States.

  - Apple (App Store) and Google (Google Play) — contracting, billing, renewal and refund of the subscriptions made in the application.

  - RevenueCat — management of the subscriptions contracted in the stores (informs GT of the Plan contracted and the status of the subscription).

  - Asaas — payment gateway for the Business Account services contracted through the website (card and PIX processing, issuance of charges).

  - Google and Apple — SSO authentication when the User chooses to log in with those accounts.

  - Sendgrid (Twilio) and Resend — sending of transactional e-mails (welcome, password recovery, notifications).

  - Brevo — sending of marketing e-mails (only to Users who have consented).

  - Make.com — operational automations (publishing on social networks, integration of workflows).

  - Sentry — application error monitoring.

  - Vercel Web Analytics — aggregated usage metrics (no cookies, no individual identification).

  - Vercel — hosting of the website and web application.

### 4.2. Public Sharing

  - Content that you publish as public (public profile, public routes, validations displayed anonymously, listings on the Shopping, including on Desapega) is visible to other Users and, in the case of the website, to visitors.

  - Contributions to the collective base of Waypoints (validations, public photos) are made available to the community, identified as "GT Community" unless you opt for attribution.

### 4.3. Sharing due to Legal Obligation

  - Compliance with a court order or a request from a competent authority (the Police, the Ministério Público (Brazil's Public Prosecutor's Office), the ANPD (Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados, Brazil's National Data Protection Authority), the Receita Federal (Brazil's Federal Revenue Service), etc.).

  - Defense of GT's rights in court or in administrative proceedings.

  - Compliance with tax, consumer and data protection legislation.

### 4.4. Commercial Sharing — Business Account

  - Establishments with a Business Account, upon receiving an expression of interest from a User (e.g., a click on "learn more" about the featured point), receive anonymized information on the geographic origin of the interest, without direct identification of the User, except when the User themselves opts for direct contact.

## 5. International Data Transfer

Some of our data processing Processors (notably Microsoft Azure, Google, Apple, RevenueCat, Sendgrid, Brevo, Vercel) process data on servers located in other countries, especially in the United States.

When an international transfer occurs, we ensure that the recipient:

  - Meets adequate data protection standards (in accordance with art. 33 of the LGPD).

  - Has data processing agreements (DPA — Data Processing Agreements) with clauses that ensure a level of protection compatible with Brazilian legislation.

  - Complies with relevant international regulations (GDPR, ISO certifications, etc.).

GT prioritizes, whenever technically feasible, the processing of data in datacenters located in Brazil.

## 7. Information Security

GT adopts reasonable technical and administrative measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, and improper communication or dissemination, including:

  - Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS 1.2+) in all communications between your device and our servers.

  - Encryption at rest for sensitive data in the database.

  - Passwords stored with one-way hash algorithms (not reversible).

  - Role-based internal access control, with the principle of least privilege.

  - Continuous security monitoring and anomaly detection.

  - Regular updates of libraries and dependencies to fix vulnerabilities.

  - Periodic backups with controlled retention.

Despite these efforts, no system is 100% immune to failures. In the event of a security incident that represents a relevant risk or harm to data subjects, GT will notify the ANPD and those affected within the periods provided for in art. 48 of the LGPD.

## 10. Children and Adolescents

The GT Overlander Platform is intended for persons over 18 years of age. We do not direct services to children or adolescents and we do not intentionally collect data from minors.

Sensitive features — notably Help Overlander, the Shopping (including Desapega) and others that involve direct interaction between Users — are restricted to persons who have reached 18 full years of age.

If we identify that a minor's data has been collected without adequate parental authorization, we will proceed with immediate deletion.

If you are the legal guardian of a minor who has used the Platform without your authorization, contact dpo@gtoverlander.com.br so that we can take the appropriate measures.
