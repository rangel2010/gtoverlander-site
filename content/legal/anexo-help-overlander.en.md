## 1. Definitions and Scope

1.1. "Help Overlander" is the Platform feature that allows Users to signal to other geographically nearby Users the need for assistance in situations of mechanical breakdown, tire trouble, dead battery, lack of fuel or other difficulties during trips.

1.2. "Requester" is the User who asks for help. "Provider" is the User who offers to assist. "Interaction" is any communication or meeting arising from Help Overlander.

1.3. "Volunteer Mechanic", "Overlander Support" and other designations are descriptive identifications of the User's willingness to provide assistance. They do not constitute a regulated profession nor a guarantee of technical ability.

## 5. Recommended Precautions

Although responsibility for safety is exclusively yours, GT strongly recommends the following precautions:

  - Inform a third party you trust, before the meeting, that you have started an interaction via Help Overlander, indicating the other User's profile.

  - Whenever possible, choose a public place for the meeting (busy gas station, rest area, busy roads).

  - Avoid meetings at night, except in case of real and absolute need.

  - If you are traveling accompanied, keep your companion present during the interaction.

  - If you are a female user, consider activating the "I accept help only from women" filter, when that setting is available.

  - Carefully evaluate the other User's profile (previous ratings, history, verification) before accepting the interaction.

  - At any time, you may decline or cancel the interaction, with no need for justification.

  - Do not share financial data, passwords or documents during the interaction.

  - In a situation of serious risk to life, health or property, immediately call the competent public services (Military Police — 190; SAMU, Brazil's public emergency medical service — 192; Firefighters — 193).

6.1. The Platform provides settings that allow the User to restrict the interactions they receive, including:

  - Minimum rating required for the other User.

  - Maximum search radius.

  - Specific categories of assistance.

6.2. GT will make efforts to continuously evolve these settings based on community demands and safety best practices.

6.3. The default setting is the most restrictive. You may relax it at your discretion, under your responsibility.

## 7. Identity Verification of Providers

7.1. Users who sign up as recurring Providers (Volunteer Mechanics or equivalent designation) are encouraged to go through an identity verification process, which includes submitting an official photo ID and a selfie.

7.2. The purpose of the verification is to confirm that the person behind the profile is real and corresponds to the registration data. It does NOT replace a background check nor guarantee trustworthiness or technical ability.

7.3. Verified Providers receive a distinctive badge on their profile, visible to the Requester before acceptance.

## 8. Mandatory Mutual Rating

8.1. After each interaction started via Help Overlander, both parties will be invited to rate the experience (from 1 to 5 stars) and, optionally, leave a descriptive comment.

8.2. Systematically negative ratings, substantiated reports, abusive behavior or signs of fraud may result in:

  - Temporary suspension of use of the feature.

  - Loss of Provider status.

  - Permanent ban from Help Overlander, with the other functions of the Platform maintained.

  - Permanent ban from the Platform as a whole.

  - Other measures provided for in the Terms of Use, including the adoption of applicable judicial measures.

8.3. Untruthful ratings, motivated by bad faith or personal revenge, are also subject to sanction against the rater.

Without prejudice to the general prohibitions provided for in the Terms of Use, the following are specifically prohibited in the context of Help Overlander:

  - Requesting fictitious help, for the purpose of entertainment, attention, unrelated personal contact or any other purpose that does not correspond to the real need described.

  - Charging amounts not previously agreed between the parties.

  - Using the feature as a channel for flirting, romantic encounters, soliciting clients for non-emergency services or any other purpose unrelated to roadside assistance.

  - Promoting the sale of products or services during the interaction, except those directly related to the assistance provided.

## 10. Privacy and Location Sharing

10.1. The Help Overlander feature, by its nature, requires sharing the User's approximate (and, at some moments, precise) location with other geographically nearby Users.

10.2. The User may, at any time, deactivate their visibility in the feature through the Account settings. When it is deactivated, the User will not receive requests nor appear in the lists of nearby Providers.

10.3. Messages exchanged between Users in the context of Help Overlander requests will be recorded by GT for 12 (twelve) months, counted from the date of the request, for the exclusive purposes of:

  - Investigation of security incidents.

  - Handling of reports and moderation procedures.

  - Compliance with court orders.

10.4. GT does not actively monitor the content of the messages in real time, but reserves the right to access them when there are well-founded indications of violation of the Terms or of the law.

10.5. The processing of this personal data follows the guidelines detailed in the Platform's Privacy Policy.
