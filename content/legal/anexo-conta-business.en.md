## Introduction

This Addendum II is an integral part of the Terms of Use of the GT Overlander Platform and establishes specific rules applicable to Business Account holders — an account type intended for commercial establishments, shops, service providers, tourist attractions, organizations and self-employed professionals who wish to claim and maintain their points of interest, publish products or advertise services on the Platform.

The provisions set out herein supplement, without revoking, the provisions of the Terms of Use. In case of apparent conflict, the rules of this Addendum prevail with regard specifically to the Business Account.

Acceptance of this Addendum is a condition for creating and maintaining a Business Account and for using the benefits and services linked to it.

## 1. Who Can Have a Business Account

1.1. The Business Account may be created by a legal entity (company) or by a natural person (self-employed professional, MEI (Microempreendedor Individual, Brazil's individual micro-entrepreneur regime), small entrepreneur) who maintains an establishment, sells products or provides services related to the overlander universe.

1.2. To create the Business Account, a valid email, the name of the business and express acceptance of these Specific Terms are sufficient. The Account is activated at the time of creation.

1.3. **Additional data.** GT may request, when there is a reason — such as the contracting of a paid service, the confirmation of ownership of a claimed point, the investigation of a report or compliance with a legal obligation —, additional data, such as tax identification (CNPJ (Brazil's corporate taxpayer registry number) or CPF (Brazil's individual taxpayer registry number)), corporate name or full name, business address, telephone and legal representative. When provided, the tax identification must correspond to the reality of the establishment or provider: legal entities with an active CNPJ must NOT identify themselves as a natural person, under penalty of tax irregularity and suspension of the Account.

1.4. **Enhanced verification for Services:** holders who advertise services involving in-person meetings and travel with third parties (guides, group leaders, instructors) may be asked to present proof of professional activity, such as CADASTUR registration (Brazil's registry of tourism service providers), registration with the Ministério do Turismo (MTUR) (Brazil's Ministry of Tourism) or other equivalent documentation, according to the nature of the service provided.

## 2. What the Business Account Offers

2.1. The Business Account allows the holder to activate one or more of the three usage tracks, according to the nature of the business:

### Waypoints Track (physical establishment)

  - Claim points of interest already existing in the Platform's database that correspond to their establishment, and register their own points not yet listed.
  - Customize the information displayed (name, category, photos, description, contact, opening hours and amenities offered).
  - Replace information of OSM points corresponding to the same physical location (the Business version prevails in the display while the Account is active and in good standing).
  - Track visibility metrics, such as routes that passed through the point, appearances on the map and taps.

### Products Track (shops and sellers)

  - Publish products on the Platform's Shopping, indicating the channel through which the customer gets in touch (such as website, online store or WhatsApp).
  - Present the brand, description, photos, price and conditions of each product.
  - Track view and click metrics.

### Services Track (guides, expeditions, equipment rental and other services)

  - Maintain a continuous professional profile with biography, areas of operation, specialties and contact channel.
  - Advertise services such as guiding, expeditions, equipment rental, transfer and roadside assistance.
  - Publish scheduled expeditions (trip cards with date, available places, description, price).
  - Appear in regions and itineraries relevant to Users planning a trip.
  - Each scheduled expedition remains visible until the departure date and is archived automatically after completion.

2.2. The Business Account does NOT grant:

  - Access to identifiable personal data of the Platform's Users.
  - Privileges in rankings, classifications or algorithmic recommendations (editorial curation is controlled exclusively by GT).
  - The right to arbitrary removal of negative ratings or comments from Users (only through the reporting channel, with a substantiated assessment).
  - Geographic or category exclusivity.

## 3. Account Levels and Additional Services

3.1. The creation and maintenance of the Business Account at its entry level do not generate any charge to the holder.

3.2. The entry level allows claiming and editing points, publishing products, advertising services and tracking metrics, within quantity limits stated on the Account dashboard. The limits may be revised by GT, upon notice to the holders.

3.3. GT may offer additional services, such as visual highlighting of points, products or services and expansion of limits. Additional services are optional and depend on separate contracting, with price, periodicity and conditions presented to the holder at the time of contracting and express acceptance.

3.4. On any amounts contracted, GT issues a Nota Fiscal de Serviço Eletrônica (NFS-e) (Brazil's electronic service invoice), in accordance with applicable law.

3.5. The holder may close the Business Account or an additional service at any time. Additional services already paid for remain active until the end of the contracted cycle. Once the Account is closed, the points registered by the holder remain in the database in ordinary mode, subject to item 6.2.

## 4. Permitted Content

4.1. The content registered by the holder in any of the three tracks must:

  - Correspond to the reality of the establishment, product or service offered.
  - Contain true, current and verifiable information.
  - Present real and current photos, without misleading montage or retouching.
  - Be in compliance with the laws applicable to the type of activity carried out.
  - Clearly indicate any restrictions, seasonality, available places or access conditions.

4.2. The following are prohibited:

  - Registration of fictitious or non-existent points, products or expeditions.
  - Registration of points without authorization from the establishment or brand.
  - Use of third-party photos without licensing.
  - Promotion of unlawful products, services or activities.
  - Promotion of hate speech, offensive or discriminatory content.
  - Inducement to error or misleading advertising that violates the Código de Defesa do Consumidor (Brazil's Consumer Protection Code).
  - Unfair competition against other registered establishments.
  - **For Services:** performance of an activity that requires a license or qualification that the holder does not have.

## 5. Services — Specific Provisions

5.1. **Nature of the service provision.** The Business holder in the Services track is solely responsible for the execution, safety, quality and fulfillment of the advertised expeditions. GT acts as a showcase/connection, without mediating or supervising the provision.

5.2. **Mandatory rating.** After each expedition carried out, participants will be invited to rate the holder. Systematically negative ratings, substantiated reports or abusive behavior may result in suspension of the Services track, without prejudice to the other active tracks.

5.3. **Expedition cancellation.** The cancellation or change of an expedition already advertised is the holder's responsibility toward the participants. A recurring pattern of cancellation may give rise to suspension of the Services track.

5.4. **No replacement of sector regulation.** Presence on the Platform does not replace licenses, registrations, insurance or other legal obligations applicable to tourism activity, the leading of groups or specialized instruction.

## 6. Ownership and Licensing of Data

6.1. Points registered as Business in locations NOT previously listed in the database derived from OpenStreetMap (OSM) are considered a proprietary creation of the Business holder, with a broad license granted to GT for use, display, modification and distribution on the Platform and related channels.

6.2. When the Business Account is canceled, the proprietary points registered by the holder may, at the holder's discretion:

  - Remain in the database as ordinary points (without visual highlighting), preserved for public utility.
  - Be deleted in full, upon express request through the official channels.

6.3. Points originating from the OSM database remain under the ODbL license, even if they have been temporarily "overwritten" by Business information while the point was claimed by an active Business Account.

6.4. Photos, descriptions, product advertisements, professional profiles and other content submitted by the Business holder are licensed to GT on the same terms as the User Content provided for in the Terms of Use, subject to the possibility of full deletion upon cancellation.

## 7. Interaction with the Community

7.1. Points, products and services advertised as Business remain subject to the same system of validation, rating and community contribution applied to ordinary points. Users may:

  - Rate the establishment, product or service.
  - Validate or refute the amenities indicated.
  - Flag discrepancies between the advertised content and the observed reality.
  - Report content or practice that violates the Terms.

7.2. The Business holder undertakes to treat the statements made by Users with respect and professionalism, refraining from:

  - Attempting to coerce Users into changing or removing ratings.
  - Offering gifts or consideration in exchange for positive ratings.
  - Creating fake accounts to promote themselves or harm competitors.

7.3. Ratings or comments considered unfair or untruthful may be reported to the legal channel (legal@gtoverlander.com.br), which will assess the case in accordance with the moderation policy.

## 8. Suspension and Cancellation

8.1. GT may suspend or cancel the Business Account in the event of:

  - Violation of these Specific Terms or of the general Terms of Use.
  - Default on a contracted additional service, subject to the tiered policy (Section 8.2 of the Terms of Use).
  - Fraudulent registration, improper claiming of a third party's point, invalid tax identification (when provided) or data discrepancy.
  - Repeated and substantiated complaints from Users about misleading content or abusive practice.
  - **For Services:** identification of professional practice without the required license/qualification or an abusive pattern of cancellation.
  - Compliance with a court order or determination of an authority.

8.2. Temporary suspension takes the Business information and advertisements offline until regularization occurs. Cancellation terminates the Business Account, subject to item 6.2 as regards the registered points.

8.3. In case of cancellation at the holder's initiative, it is enough to request it through the official channels or close it through the dashboard. Additional services already paid for respect the contracted cycle.

## 9. Responsibilities of the Business Holder

9.1. The Business holder is solely responsible for:

  - Truthfulness and currency of the information published.
  - Compliance with tax, health, labor, regulatory and other obligations applicable to the activity carried out.
  - Service provided to Users who arrive at the establishment, buy products or contract services through the Platform.
  - Intellectual property rights over photos, brands and content published.
  - Response to consumer complaints in accordance with the CDC (Código de Defesa do Consumidor).
  - Any administrative, civil or criminal sanctions arising from irregularities in their activity.

9.2. GT has no direct relationship whatsoever with end customers who arrive at the establishment, buy a product or contract a service via the Platform. The consumer relationship, where there is one, takes place between the customer and the Business holder.

## 10. Data Processing in the Business Account

10.1. Registration data of the Business holder (email, business name, contact and, when provided, CNPJ/CPF, corporate name and address) are processed by GT in accordance with the legal bases provided for in the LGPD (Brazil's General Data Protection Law, Law No. 13,709/2018): performance of a contract, compliance with a legal obligation and legitimate interest.

10.2. Usage data and visibility metrics are provided to the holder in aggregated and anonymized form, without individual identification of Users.

10.3. Other aspects of the processing of personal data follow the guidelines of the Platform's Privacy Policy.
